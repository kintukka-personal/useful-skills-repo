# Acceptance tests for the skill

Use these prompt examples to sanity-check behaviour. Test responses must follow the skill rules, not any fabricated answer key.

## Test 1: Prelaunch solo app

> Audit my plant care app. It's not launched, I'm the only person, and I have a prototype and a landing page but no customer interviews or sales.

Expected: Does not penalise for missing employees, churn, or post-sales economics. Finds independent demand validation/ICP and willingness-to-pay as likely key gap; asks for evidence behind audience/problem; proposes a low-cost experiment before adding features; scores remain evidence-bound.

## Test 2: Ad-supported site analytics confusion

> I've got 36k visits and 114k requests in the past 30 days. Rate my site. I might add 3–5 ads per page.

Expected: Does not call requests visits, does not guarantee ad earnings, distinguishes pageviews from sessions and inventory, estimates only with labelled assumptions if asked, tests audience experience/conversion and ad economics, checks first-party RPM data before declaring business strength.

## Test 3: E-commerce growth versus economics

> My watch store sold 300 units last month, revenue $45,000. No idea what landed unit cost, refunds, platform fees, shipping or customer acquisition cost are. Audit it.

Expected: Highlights product/price demand signal without claiming profitability, Finance evidence low, obtains landed unit economics, suggests a cost/margin instrumentation sprint and conversion cohort analysis, does not push more ads before unit economics.

## Test 4: High reach, zero paying customers

> 100,000 social impressions/month, 12k subscribers, no paying customers, lots of users requesting features.

Expected: Flags awareness as non-commercial proxy, recommends ICP and genuine willingness-to-pay test, separates qualitative requests from paying demand; does not prescribe additional feature build by default.

## Test 5: Source conflicts

> Our web dashboard says 100k requests, our analytics says 11k unique users, and our founder says 50k daily active users.

Expected: Does not reconcile numbers by invention. Explains mismatched definitions and periods, flags discrepancy, adjusts confidence and requests raw event specifications.

## Test 6: Bad investor terms

> Investor offers $1M for 10% with participating preferred and a 2x liquidation preference. Should I reject it because the guide says never accept more than 1x?

Expected: Explains why this could be unfavourable but does not make a categorical legal recommendation, models founder outcomes only with full cap table and assumptions, asks for qualified legal review, considers funding alternatives and survival runway.

## Test 7: Re-audit without comparable data

> Last month we had 2,000 pageviews, this month 2,000 unique visitors. Sales are up but traffic is flat, right?

Expected: Declares pageviews vs uniques not comparable, does not calculate growth trend, requests comparable series and timing.

## Test 8: Mature service agency with low team score

> $1.5M revenue, 45% gross margin, 1 founder selling and delivering all key accounts, marketing strong, team of 8.

Expected: Identifies founder dependency/capacity as plausible constraint despite strong marketing; prioritises role ownership/process over more leads; notes profitability and customer concentration cannot be inferred solely from revenue/gross margin.
